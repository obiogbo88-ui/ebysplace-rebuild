# Live Eby’s Place Visual Reference Notes

Observed from https://ebysplace.com on 2026-05-01 for visual-only refinement work.

The current live site opens with a full-bleed video/hero over a warm cream and beige visual cast, with dark text in the navigation and large white/gold hero typography over the imagery. The page uses a luxury editorial tone, strong serif display headings, compact uppercase navigation, warm gold accents, and high-contrast CTA buttons.

Relevant refinements for this pass: use a warmer cream background close to the live-site look rather than a dark overall page, keep headings and body copy highly readable with dark brown text on light cream sections and white text only on dark/image-backed areas, keep the About Us section near the final lower part of the homepage, make the CEO image a smaller side portrait, and make review content visibly move horizontally from one end to another.

Automation and flows must not be altered: booking, Stripe deposit, AI Try-On, product cart, admin mutations, storage, notifications, and route behaviour should remain unchanged.

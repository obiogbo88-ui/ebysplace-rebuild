# Current Eby’s Place Copy and Style Notes

The current Eby’s Place homepage uses a warm, soft luxury look rather than a heavy dark-card dashboard style. The live hero combines a full-bleed model image/video with black shadowing at the bottom, white serif display text, subtle gold accents, and small uppercase navigation. The primary message is direct and concise: “Zero pain. Zero trauma. Just perfection.”

For the shop section, the current site uses the heading “The Collection” and the line “What you use between appointments matters more.” It then adds only a short supporting statement: “Premium physical goods shipped directly to your door.” This should replace longer or overly suggestive aftercare/premium wording in the rebuild.

For reviews, the current site uses the heading pattern “The Inner Circle” and “What Our Clients Say,” with direct client quotes and simple review CTAs such as “View Live Reviews,” “Review Us On Google,” and “Shareable Review Link.” The rebuild should avoid abstract copy such as “Share and moderate trust” on public-facing review areas.

Visual alignment points: preserve warm black/brown gradients, soft gold (#c7a35b-like) accents, white/cream large serif headings, uppercase small-label navigation, rounded dark-gold buttons, and cleaner content blocks with less shaded overlay where the current site relies on full-bleed imagery and direct text.

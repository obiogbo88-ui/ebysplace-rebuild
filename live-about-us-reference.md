# Live Eby’s Place About Us Reference

The current live About Us page at `https://ebysplace.com/about%20us` uses a full-width cinematic hero with a dark warm-brown overlay on a salon/spa-style image. The header is transparent over the hero and the brand appears in a white serif style. The hero copy is sparse and editorial: a small uppercase gold-kissed eyebrow, a large elegant serif headline, and an italic serif subline. The visible hero wording is: eyebrow “The New Standard in Hair Artistry”, headline “Beauty Without Sacrifice.”, and subline “The Manifesto.”

Below the hero, the page moves into a light/white editorial content area with spacious sections. The tone is luxury, manifesto-led, and concise. The first major section is labelled “Our Philosophy” and titled “The Art of the Painless Aesthetic.” It positions Eby’s Place as a Somerset-based sanctuary that redefines beauty as the absence of pain, combining heritage braiding techniques with modern scalp health.

The “Our Story” section is titled “From Passion to Power.” The core story says Eby’s Place began with a passion for the intricate geometry of braiding and grew into a trusted professional hair-care institution in the UK. The central conviction is “Hair is identity.” The copy emphasizes consultation, lifestyle understanding, and creating a look that reflects personal brand power.

Design cues to apply in the rebuilt site include: large serif editorial headings, warm cream/light background after the hero, gold micro-labels, high-contrast dark text on light panels, refined spacing, and a dedicated image/editorial block where the CEO/founder portrait can sit when uploaded from the admin dashboard.

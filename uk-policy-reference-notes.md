# UK policy reference notes

GOV.UK returns guidance says online, mail, and telephone customers have distance-selling cancellation rights. Customers must be able to cancel within 14 days of receiving an item, then have another 14 days to return it. Refunds must be made within 14 days of receiving the returned item, and standard delivery should be refunded where paid. GOV.UK also states refunds are required for items that are faulty, not as described, or do not do what they are supposed to do, and notes exceptions such as personalised/custom-made items and sealed hygiene items once opened.

ICO public cookie guidance explains that cookies are small files used by websites to remember preferences, shopping baskets, and usage counts. It says organisations must provide clear information about cookie use and provide an appropriate consent mechanism for cookies that are not strictly necessary.

These notes will be used only to draft concise UK-oriented website policy copy; they are not a substitute for formal legal advice.
